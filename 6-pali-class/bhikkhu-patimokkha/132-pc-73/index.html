    PC 73 mohanasikkhāpadaṃ¶

    yo pana bhikkhu anvaḍḍhamāsaṃ pātimokkhe uddissamāne evaṃ vadeyya, idān'eva kho ahaṃ jānāmi, ayam'pi kira dhammo sutt'āgato suttapariyāpanno anvaḍḍhamāsaṃ uddesaṃ āgacchatī'ti.¶
