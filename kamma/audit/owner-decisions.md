# Owner decisions for audits (study-tools)

Project-specific rulings that every audit in this repo must respect. Rejected patterns: do not raise them again. Rulings: treat the ruled form as correct. Source: the owner's review of `pat-audit/pat-audit-tsv.md` (commit `519a7ca` and edits of 2026-11-12). Items marked *inferred* come from deletions without a stated reason; ask if a case is borderline.

## Rejected patterns (do not report)

- Same root written with different sense labels: `√har (live)` / `√har (carry)`, `√dus (be angry, corrupt)` / `√dus (hate, dislike)`.
- Root spelling variants where DPD keeps both: `√sajj` / `√saj`. *inferred*
- Optative / future / imperative / absolutive constructions ending in `+ ti` instead of the real ending. *inferred*
- "Decide one convention" items with no error: one label for absolutives (`verb/ger`, `ind/abs` …), homonym numbers in the Pāli cell (`tato 2`), sandhi rows without member rows or meaning, `iti` tagged `adv`, headings with `-īti` without apostrophe, a space in a source name (`methunadhamma sikkhāpadaṃ`).
- Lemma changes away from the DPD headword form (`rāja` → `rājan`); lemma reanalyses not backed by DPD (`anaññaṃ`, `atikkāmayato`). *inferred*
- "Check edition" items with no edition evidence (`avacaṃ` after `apassaṃ`, `taṃkhaṇikāyapi`). *inferred*
- Case-marker words in front of a locative adjective gloss ("in belonging to the community") when the case is right. *inferred*
- Reanalyses rejected: `ajānamevāhaṃ` as `ajānaṃ + eva + ahaṃ`; `gāhaṃ` adj inside `hatthagāhaṃ`; `vatthuṃ` nt nom sg; `anussāvitaṃ` noun → pp. *inferred*

## Rulings (the ruled form is correct)

- `saddheyyavacasā`: `kammadhāraya` everywhere.
- `chattapāṇissa`, `daṇḍapāṇissa`, `satthapāṇissa`, `āvudhapāṇissa` (and like compounds of persons): `kammadhāraya > bahubbīhi`.
- `kattikapuṇṇamaṃ`: `kammadhāraya`.
- `eḷakaloma`: nt.
- `vatthuṃ desetabbaṃ` (with `ṃ`) in both rules.
- Quote before `ti`: straight `'ti`, not `’ti`.
- Pārājika: "defeat" / "defeated" everywhere.
- Spelling `Pātimokkha` in English glosses (not `Patimokkha`).
- `rājāno` (PA 2): "authorities", not "king's men".
- `bālo`: "fool; idiot; immature person".
- `agilāno`: "not sick; healthy".
